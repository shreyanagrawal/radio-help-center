Refund Policy

Thank you for choosing our services. We are committed to delivering high-quality professional solutions. Please read this Refund Policy carefully before purchasing any of our services.

Our services include, but are not limited to:

  • IT Consulting

  • Software Development
  • Technical Support Services
  • Business Process Outsourcing (BPO)
  • Managed Services

Because these are customized professional services, refunds are limited as described below.

2. Before Work Begins

If you cancel your order before any work has started, you may be eligible for a full refund, excluding any payment processing or administrative charges, where applicable.

3. After Work Has Started

Once work has commenced, payments made are generally non-refundable because resources, time, and expertise have already been allocated to your project.

If only part of the project has been completed, we may, at our sole discretion, consider a partial refund based on the amount of work completed.

4. Milestone Payments

  • Completed milestones are non-refundable.
  • Future unpaid milestones may be canceled by either party according to the service agreement.

5. Monthly Support or Managed Services

Monthly or recurring service fees are non-refundable once the service period has begun.

Cancellation will stop future billing where applicable but does not entitle the client to a refund for the current billing period.

6. Client Delays

Refunds will not be issued for delays caused by:

  • Failure to provide required information or access.
  • Failure to respond within a reasonable time.
  • Changes in project scope after work has begun.
  • Delays caused by third-party vendors or service providers.

7. Third-Party Products and Services

Payments made for domain registration, web hosting, software licenses, cloud services, SSL certificates, APIs, or other third-party services are non-refundable once purchased.

8. Duplicate Payments

If you are accidentally charged more than once for the same service, the duplicate payment will be refunded after verification.

9. Exceptions

We may review refund requests on a case-by-case basis if exceptional circumstances exist. Any approved refund remains at our sole discretion.

10. Contact

For refund-related questions, please contact our support team through the contact information provided on our website.
